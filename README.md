## 🛠️ Systems I've designed and built

| System | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **[HireDesk — Recruitment Automation](https://audreasisy.vercel.app/work/hiredesk)** | Takes a role from "we need to hire" to a signed probation contract: one-click role setup, CV intake matched to open roles, bilingual email drafts, calculated offers, contract and NDA. | Live. 6 roles run end to end; 39 matched CVs filed in one intake run |
| **[Document Automation — POs & Completion Minutes](https://audreasisy.vercel.app/work/pocm)** | Generates bilingual (VN/EN) Purchase Orders and Completion Minutes from one master workbook: live totals, auto-filed, logged, safe to regenerate. | Live team-wide. ~25–50 minutes per order → one click |
| **[Cash Flow Forecast Automation](https://audreasisy.vercel.app/work/cashflow)** | A weekly forecast that pulls live from 4 teams' source files, times each payment to the week the cash lands and flags negative weeks. | In final review. 125 weeks, weekly instead of monthly |

**How I build:** human decisions stay human, mechanical work is automated. Emails are drafts, never auto-sent. Rules live in one place. Every real-world failure becomes a permanent fix.

*Built with an AI coding assistant working to my design and spec. I review, test and debug every change; the architecture and decisions are mine.*
